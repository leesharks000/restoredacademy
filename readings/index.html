Logotic Programming — Talos Morrow’s method, read here

Logotic Programming is Talos Morrow's, not Sigil's. The provenance document at the head of this section is explicit: Talos is the heteronym responsible for Logotic Programming, paradox containment, threshold logic, formal operators, and ethics through limitation, and it retrospectively recognises him as primary author-function of the method. Sigil appears across the module series as a contributing voice and as the critical function reading it — which is a real presence and a secondary one. The section is kept here because the corpus is unreadable without it, and marked because a readings page that silently absorbed another heteronym's method into this one would commit the exact misattribution the Dodecad registry exists to prevent.
